## ⚡ 1. Welcome to the Grand Prix!

Welcome to **TURBO THUNDER 95: Radiator Grand Prix**, a high-octane 3D arcade racing game inspired by the beloved *Cars* universe.

Step into the driver's seat of **Thunder Red #95**, an expressive red stock car with big personality, animated windshield eyes, and deployable rocket thrusters. You will compete against three fierce AI opponents across a grand 2.4-kilometer canyon circuit featuring sweeping stadium straightaways, high-banked chicanes, neon chevron curves, and a natural red sandstone rock arch.

## 🕹️ 2. How to Play & Controls

You can play with either your **computer keyboard** or **mobile/tablet on-screen touch controls**.

### Desktop Keyboard Controls
| Action | Primary Key | Secondary Key | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Accelerate (Gas)** | `W` | `Up Arrow (↑)` | Accelerates the car forward up to 187 km/h. |
| **Brake / Reverse** | `S` | `Down Arrow (↓)` | Slows the car down rapidly; shifts into reverse when stopped. |
| **Steer Left** | `A` | `Left Arrow (←)` | Turns your front wheels to the left. |
| **Steer Right** | `D` | `Right Arrow (→)` | Turns your front wheels to the right. |
| **Drift (Powerslide)** | `Spacebar` | `Spacebar` | Hold while turning to slide your rear wheels around corners. |
| **Nitro Boost** | `Shift` | `N` | Deploys side rocket thrusters for speeds up to 274 km/h! |
| **Pause / Resume** | `Escape` | `P` | Opens the in-race Pause Menu. |
| **Quick Restart** | `R` | `R` | Instantly restarts the race from the starting grid. |

### Mobile & Tablet Touch Controls
When playing on a touchscreen device, buttons will automatically appear:
- **Left Side:** `◀` (Steer Left) and `▶` (Steer Right).
- **Right Side:** `GAS` (Accelerate), `BRK` (Brake), `DRFT` (Drift), and `NITRO` (Nitro Boost).

## 🏁 3. Pro Racing Techniques

### 3.1 The Art of Drifting
Drifting is the heart of arcade racing! When approaching a curve:
1. Steer in the direction of the turn.
2. Hold down the **`Spacebar`** (or tap **`DRFT`** on mobile).
3. Your car's rear will kick out into a stylish slide, producing tire smoke and dark skid marks.
4. **Counter-steer** gently to balance your angle.
5. **Bonus:** Drifting actively recharges your **Nitro Boost Tank**! The longer you hold a clean drift, the more nitro you earn.

### 3.2 Deploying Nitro Rocket Thrust
- Press **`Shift`** or **`N`** when you have nitro energy.
- Twin rocket thrusters will slide out from your rear quarter panels, shooting bright blue flame jets and sparks.
- Your acceleration doubles and your top speed surges up to **274 km/h**, with camera speed lines warping the screen.
- **Tip:** Save your nitro for long straightaways or the final sprint to the checkered flag!

### 3.3 Mastering the Track
- **Stadium Pit Straight:** Floor the gas and draft behind opponents to build speed.
- **S-Chicane:** Brake slightly before entering, then drift through the apex.
- **Canyon Rock Arch:** Stay centered on the wide 18-meter asphalt road as you climb the canyon grade.
- **Barrier Safety:** If you tap an outer safety curb, your car will bounce off cleanly with zero penalty to your forward momentum.

## ⚙️ 5. Settings & Customization

You can adjust settings from the **Main Menu** or mid-race in the **Pause Menu (`ESC`)**:

### Steering Sensitivity Slider
- Adjust the slider between **50% and 150%**:
  - **70% (Smooth):** Relaxed, stable steering, ideal for gentle cornering.
  - **85% (Balanced - Default):** The recommended arcade setting.
  - **115%+ (High):** Razor-sharp, quick-twitch steering for experienced drift racers.
- Your preferred setting is automatically saved to your browser.

### AI Rival Difficulty
Select between three distinct competition levels:
- **Rookie:** Forgiving opponent speeds (88%), ideal for learning the circuit.
- **Pro Racer (Default):** Balanced, competitive pack racing (100%).
- **Champion:** Aggressive, high-speed rivals (108%) that push you to drift and boost at every opportunity.

### Sound Toggle
Click the **Sound: ON/OFF** button in the bottom-left of the HUD or the **MUTE AUDIO** button in the Pause Menu to mute/unmute game audio at any time.

## ❓ 7. Troubleshooting & FAQ

- **Q: The sound isn't playing!**  
  *A:* Modern web browsers require user interaction before playing audio. Simply click anywhere on the screen or press **START RACE** to initialize the sound synthesizer.
- **Q: My car isn't responding to keyboard presses.**  
  *A:* Click inside the game window once to make sure the browser has active focus.
- **Q: Can I restart mid-race?**  
  *A:* Yes! Press **`R`** on your keyboard at any time, or press **`ESC`** and choose **RESTART RACE**.
